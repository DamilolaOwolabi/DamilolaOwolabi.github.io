I'm an Associate Distribution Engineer at **Leidos** in Houston, where I support utility clients including CenterPoint Energy and Duke Energy on medium- and low-voltage distribution design. My work covers load calculations, short circuit analysis, underground duct bank engineering, and QA/QC on design packages.

I hold a **BS in Electrical Engineering** from Texas A&M University and an **MS in Data Science** from Southern Methodist University. That pairing is where I do my best work: I know how the grid is built, and I know how to model the data it produces.

I'm focused on **energy analytics, grid analytics, and quantitative roles** where engineering judgment meets data science. My [projects](/projects/) range from predictive modeling and interactive visualization to drone-based crop analytics and organic photovoltaics research.
