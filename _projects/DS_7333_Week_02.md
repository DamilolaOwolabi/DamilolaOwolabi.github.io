## Why this app

The lecture covered Lasso and Ridge as two ways to fight overfitting, and I wanted a practical rule for picking one. This app puts both side by side on the same data so the trade-off is visible: how many features each keeps, and what that costs in accuracy. Everything runs in your browser.
