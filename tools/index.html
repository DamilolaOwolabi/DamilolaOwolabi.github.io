Formulas and assumptions
  • Load kVA = kW ÷ power factor. Required kVA = load kVA × (1 + spare capacity).
  • Standard ratings follow common ANSI/IEEE distribution sizes: single-phase 10 to 500 kVA, three-phase 45 to 2,500 kVA.
  • Full-load current: I = kVA × 1000 ÷ V (single-phase, line-to-line V) or I = kVA × 1000 ÷ (√3 × V) (three-phase).
  • Available fault current (bolted, at the secondary terminals) = full-load current ÷ (%Z ÷ 100), assuming an infinite utility source. Real values are lower once source and conductor impedance are included, so treat this as an upper bound.

For learning and quick checks only, not a substitute for a stamped design or your utility's standards.
